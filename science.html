Why Micro-Infusion?

Precision
beyond
conventional
application.

Traditional topical products are applied to the scalp surface. Riavâte uses controlled 0.5 mm micro-infusion to create temporary micro-channels during application, providing a more targeted approach to scalp care.

  1. 01

    Precision micro-channels

    0.5 mm micro-infusion creates controlled micro-channels during application.

  2. 02

    Targeted application

    Cuprofuse® is applied directly across the targeted scalp area.

  3. 03

    Formula + device

    The micro-infusion device and concentrated serum are designed to work as one considered system.

Research Spotlight

What does the
published
evidence show?

Microneedling has been investigated in clinical research on androgenetic alopecia.

In a 2013 randomized evaluator-blinded study involving 100 participants, researchers compared weekly 1.5 mm microneedling plus 5% minoxidil against 5% minoxidil alone for 12 weeks.

+91.4

Mean hair-count change

Reported in the microneedling + minoxidil group after 12 weeks in the Dhurat et al. study.

Important Context

This research investigated 1.5 mm microneedling combined with minoxidil — not the Riavâte 0.5 mm system. The study is presented as background research on microneedling and should not be interpreted as a clinical result for Riavâte.
