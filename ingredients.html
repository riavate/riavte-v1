Every ingredient has a purpose — from supporting scalp condition and hydration to maintaining the performance, stability and experience of the formula.

The 19 ingredients

Purposefully selected

Each ingredient in Cuprofuse® has been carefully selected to play a considered role within the formula — bringing together peptides, scalp-care actives, botanical extracts and conditioning ingredients in one concentrated serum.

  • 01 — Copper Tripeptide-1

    GHK-Cu · The hero peptide

    The leading peptide at the heart of Cuprofuse®. Selected to anchor our peptide-led approach to modern scalp care.

    Why we use it

    To provide Cuprofuse® with its defining copper-peptide component and support the formula's scalp-conditioning strategy.

  • 02 — Niacinamide

    Scalp barrier support

    A versatile form of vitamin B3 widely used in modern cosmetic skincare.

    Why we use it

    To help condition the scalp and support its natural barrier, creating a well-cared-for environment for healthier-looking hair.

  • 03 — Caffeine

    Scalp-care active

    One of the more extensively studied cosmetic ingredients in topical hair/scalp formulations, although systematic reviews note limitations in the overall quality of clinical evidence.

    Why we use it

    Selected as a targeted scalp-care active to complement Cuprofuse®'s multi-active approach.

  • 04 — Adenosine

    Hair-care active

    Adenosine has been investigated in topical hair research, including randomized studies and more recent systematic review evidence.

    Why we use it

    Included as a research-backed component within our broader cosmetic scalp-care formula.

  • 05 — Panthenol

    Condition + hydration

    Pro-vitamin B5 is widely used as a hair- and skin-conditioning ingredient and humectant.

    Why we use it

    To help moisturise and condition the scalp and hair while supporting a comfortable application experience.

  • 06 — Panax Ginseng Root Extract

    Botanical support

    A botanical extract with a long history of cosmetic use.

    Why we use it

    Selected as part of Cuprofuse®'s botanical complex to complement the formula's scalp-conditioning approach.

  • 07 — Eclipta Prostrata Extract

    Botanical complex

    A plant-derived extract used in cosmetic hair and scalp formulations.

    Why we use it

    Included as part of the selected botanical system supporting the overall scalp-care formula.

  • 08 — Licorice Root Extract

    Soothing botanical

    Glycyrrhiza glabra is widely used in cosmetics and has published research across topical skincare applications. PubMed

    Why we use it

    To complement the formula with a botanical ingredient selected for scalp-conditioning and soothing-oriented cosmetic care.

  • 09 — Camellia Sinensis Leaf Extract

    Green tea extract

    A botanical widely used in cosmetic formulations.

    Why we use it

    Selected to contribute antioxidant-oriented botanical support to the overall scalp-care formula.

  • 10 — Acetyl Tetrapeptide-3

    Targeted peptide

    A cosmetic peptide incorporated into modern hair and scalp formulations.

    Why we use it

    To complement GHK-Cu and strengthen the peptide-led identity of the Cuprofuse® formula.

  • 11 — Biotin

    Soothing botanical

    Glycyrrhiza glabra is widely used in cosmetics and has published research across topical skincare applications. PubMed

    Why we use it

    To complement the formula with a botanical ingredient selected for scalp-conditioning and soothing-oriented cosmetic care.

  • 12 — Salicylic Acid

    Scalp clarity

    A well-established cosmetic ingredient used for skin/scalp conditioning and exfoliation-related functions depending on formulation and concentration. Cosmetic safety depends on compliant concentration and formulation. PubMed

    Why we use it

    To support a cleaner, refined scalp environment as part of the complete formula.

  • 13 — Arginine

    Amino acid

    An amino acid used in cosmetic skin and hair formulations.

    Why we use it

    Included as a supporting conditioning component within Cuprofuse®'s balanced formula.

Inside Cuprofuse®

Every part of the formula has a purpose.

Because a sophisticated formula isn't built from hero actives alone. Supporting ingredients help determine hydration, texture, delivery, stability and overall product performance.

  • 14 — Aqua (Water)

    Formula base

    Provides the aqueous foundation that carries the water-compatible components of Cuprofuse®.

  • 15 — Butylene Glycol

    Humectant + solvent

    Helps retain moisture while supporting the distribution and sensory feel of the formula.

  • 16 — Propylene Glycol

    Solvent + humectant

    Helps dissolve and carry selected formula components while contributing to moisture retention.

  • 17 — 1,2-Hexanediol

    Formula support

    A multifunctional cosmetic ingredient used to support formulation performance and preservation.

  • 18 — Hydroxyacetophenone

    Formula protection

    Included to support antioxidant/preservation performance and overall formula stability.

  • 19 — Sodium Citrate

    Formula balance

    Used to help maintain the appropriate pH environment and overall balance of the formula.

Why 19?

Formulated as a system.

Cuprofuse® isn't defined by one ingredient alone.

GHK-Cu leads the formula, supported by peptides, targeted cosmetic actives, botanical extracts, conditioning ingredients and carefully selected formulation components.

Each has a different role. Together, they create the complete Cuprofuse® formula.
