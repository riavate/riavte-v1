Frequently asked questions

Answers about the Riavâte system, Cuprofuse®, and your order.

What is the Riavâte Micro-Infusion System?

Riavâte is a precision scalp-care system combining controlled 0.5 mm micro-infusion technology with Cuprofuse®, our concentrated serum led by GHK-Cu (Copper Tripeptide-1) and formulated with selected scalp-care ingredients.

What is Cuprofuse®?

Cuprofuse® is Riavâte’s concentrated cosmetic serum formulation.

Why does Riavâte use 0.5 mm micro-infusion?

The system is designed for controlled, targeted and consistent application of the Cuprofuse® serum across the scalp.
